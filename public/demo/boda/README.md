# Imágenes de ejemplo de boda

Imágenes creadas con la herramienta integrada `image_gen` para la demo de Cheetah Invite. Representan una pareja y un lugar ficticios. Se optimizaron a WebP para servirlas desde el proyecto.

## sofia-mateo.webp

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cover photograph for a fictional wedding invitation demo on a website. Create a tasteful editorial wedding photograph of a fictional adult couple, bride in an elegant ivory dress and groom in a dark suit, holding hands naturally in a lush garden courtyard of an old Mexican hacienda at golden hour. They are the only two people, photographed candidly in a medium-wide full-body composition, centered with some space around them. Natural smiles, realistic skin texture, detailed fabric, soft warm sunlight and restrained greenery and white flowers. Portrait 2:3 composition, designed to crop beautifully in a phone cover; keep both faces in the middle third and the lower foreground relatively quiet for overlaid website text. No text, logos, watermarks, borders or UI.

## celebracion.webp

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: venue photograph for the same fictional wedding invitation demo. An intimate outdoor wedding reception in the garden courtyard of a beautiful Mexican hacienda, warm stone arches, trees and softly glowing string lights at sunset, elegant wooden tables set with white linens, clear glassware and simple white floral centerpieces. Wide editorial photograph from standing eye level, natural warm light and realistic material textures, refined but welcoming atmosphere. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no borders or UI. Landscape 3:2 composition.

## ramo.webp

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wedding gallery detail photograph for a fictional wedding invitation demo. Close-up editorial still life of a bridal bouquet with ivory roses, delicate small white flowers and fresh eucalyptus, resting naturally on a softly folded ivory linen cloth on a warm stone bench in a hacienda garden. Gentle late afternoon sunlight, soft natural shadows, realistic delicate petals and linen texture, elegant quiet mood and shallow depth of field. No people, no text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no borders or UI. Portrait 2:3 composition.
